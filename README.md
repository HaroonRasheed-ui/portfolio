## 🚀 Flagship Systems & Projects

### 1. [HaeirLens / IDP Studio](https://haroonrasheed-ui.github.io/portfolio/)
- **Stack:** FastAPI, PaddleOCR, OpenVINO, OpenCV
- **What it does:** Industrial-grade document intelligence pipeline developed for a major appliance manufacturer. Processes unstructured multi-page PDFs, extracts structured invoice and warranty data, and cut processing time down to **15–20 seconds** per document.

### 2. [AI Executive Assistant (M365 On-Premises)](https://haroonrasheed-ui.github.io/portfolio/)
- **Stack:** FastAPI, Microsoft 365 Graph API, Faster-Whisper, PostgreSQL, pgvector (HNSW)
- **What it does:** 100% private, on-premises executive copilot that summarizes email priority feeds, generates daily morning briefings, and creates structured meeting minutes from audio recordings without cloud data egress.

### 3. [AI Smart Glasses for Visually Impaired People](https://github.com/HaroonRasheed-ui/AI-Smart-Glasses-Visually-Impaired-People)
- **Stack:** YOLOv8, PaddleOCR, GPS, Text-to-Speech (English & Urdu)
- **What it does:** Wearable assistive research system providing real-time spatial obstacle detection, printed text reading, and geolocation guidance via bilingual voice prompts.

### 4. [LeadIntel.AI — B2B Lead Enrichment](https://github.com/HaroonRasheed-ui/b2b-lead-enrichment-frontend)
- **Stack:** FastAPI, React, TypeScript, Gemini 2.0, Claude Haiku
- **What it does:** Autonomous intelligence pipeline that crawls company websites and LinkedIn profiles to discover their enterprise ERP footprint and technology stack with automatic fallback routing.

### 5. [n8n QBO Bookkeeping Automation Suite](https://haroonrasheed-ui.github.io/portfolio/)
- **Stack:** n8n, QuickBooks Online API, LLM Categorization Agent
- **What it does:** 5 production workflows for a US accounting firm automating transaction categorization, bank reconciliation, and low-confidence exception routing.
